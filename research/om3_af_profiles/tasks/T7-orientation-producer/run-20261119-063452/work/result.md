6. **Findings:**

1. STATIC. The orientation producer is the accelerometer on I2C channel 6, device `0x6b`. The probe `0x6006a791` accepts register `0x0F` equal to `0x70`. The sample issue `0x6006a8ab` reads 14 bytes from register `0x20` into `0x6298d7c0`.
2. STATIC. The completion `0x6006a8ca` unpacks accelerometer X, Y, Z from bytes 8, 10, 12 into `0x6298d7ce`, `0x6298d7d0`, `0x6298d7d2`. The copy `0x6006a9c1` moves them to `0x6298d758`, `0x6298d75a`, `0x6298d75c` while `0x6298d7b0` is 1.
3. STATIC. `PER_ROTATE` stores raw codes 0..5 at `0xa32c5690` and the reported index at `0xa32c568c`. Raw 0/2 are the Y signs, 1/3 the X signs, 4/5 the Z signs. The reported index is 1, 2, 3, 4 for raw 0, 1, 2, 3, and for raw 4 or 5 it keeps 2 or 4, otherwise 1.
4. DATA. The halfword table at `0x60357e68` is 11, 1, 6, 3, 8. The getter `0x60077de1` returns the entry selected by `0xa32c568c`, or 1 when `0xa32c5688` is 1.
5. OBSERVED-EMU. Each injected sample reached `0xa32c5690` through the native issue call, the native completion, the native copy, and the native store at `0x6007805e`. Sensor (0, −16384, 0) → raw 0, index 1, EXIF 1. (16384, 0, 0) → raw 1, index 2, EXIF 6. (0, 16384, 0) → raw 2, index 3, EXIF 3. (−16384, 0, 0) → raw 3, index 4, EXIF 8. (0, 0, ±16384) → raw 4 and 5.
6. OBSERVED-EMU. `0xe91` indices 0–7 stayed 0 for every pose. The pose the bank selector uses is `0xa32c5690` / `0xa32c568c`, not `0xe91`.
7. OBSERVED-EMU. OK on AF Target Mode sets `0x1db1` index 0 to 1. OK on AF Target Point sets `0x1dc1` index 0 to 1. A second OK clears the same cell. Both were 1 for the linked measurement and 0 for the control.
8. OBSERVED-EMU. With both enables 1, landscape writes `0x99a1` and `0x5962` index 1, the EXIF 8 pose writes index 3, and the EXIF 6 pose writes index 5. Those are the change indices `AFON-MENU-RESEARCH.md` §1 names normal, CCW, and CW. `0x88b2` index 0 follows the live position in every pose.
9. OBSERVED-EMU. With both enables 0, all three poses write `0x5962` index 1 and the getters read `0x99a1` index 1 and `0x5962` index 1.
10. STATIC. `0x609b3dfb` reads `0x1db1` index 0 and, when it is 1, reads `0x99a1` at 0, 2, and 4 together, or at 1, 3, and 5 when `0x5991` index 0 is 1 and `0xe1f1` index 0 is not 0. It does not select one index from the pose.
11. OBSERVED-EMU. The ten bytes at `0xa33ca384` stayed 0. The overlay bank addresses `0xa031eaa0`, `0xa031eab0`, and `0xa031eac0` are absent from the stock image. The stock image does reference `0xa33ca384` at `0x60902603`.
12. DATA. Every listing hex column matches the stock image. `work/out/listing-bytes-s4.txt` reports 0 mismatches, including the ten-byte listings.

8. **Tests and outcomes:**

- PASS. Six sensor axes each produced a distinct raw code or the documented lens-up/down code, and the unpacked halfwords matched the injected sample.
- PASS. With Orientation Linked on, the three poses wrote three different change indices, 1, 3, and 5.
- PASS. With Orientation Linked off, all three poses wrote index 1.
- PASS. Listing bytes against the stock image, 0 mismatches.
- BOUNDARY. Raw 4 and raw 5 do not select a portrait bank. The reported index stays 1 unless it was already 2 or 4.
- BOUNDARY. The direct screen opens on All. The arrow does not move a point until the main dial has selected a small target. `bank_s5` measured the All screen and also toggled the two checks back off; `bank_s6` is the measurement with the checks left on.
- BOUNDARY. The ten-byte record at `0xa33ca384` was not written by the direct screen. The per-pose index is the settings change index, not a byte in that record.

9. **New versus previously established:** Findings 1–4 confirm `hle-gaps.md` 4.7.3 and the E147 note in `models.mjs`, against this image, and add the raw-code store and the EXIF table bytes. Finding 5 is new and confirms the axis order 1, −2, 3 in that note. Finding 6 confirms T3b finding 5 and shows the real pose variable. Finding 11 confirms that the T5 bank addresses belong to the overlay, not this stock image.

10. **Remaining uncertainties.** The completion is entered from the lab after the register read, because the stock driver's transfer-done call is inside the unmodelled bus. The completion body is native. Which physical turn of the camera body the firmware calls CCW versus CW is the index name in `AFON-MENU-RESEARCH.md` §1, not a new body measurement. The stock function that would copy a bank into the ten bytes at `0xa33ca384` was not on the direct-screen path. `0x609b3dfb` reads three indices at once; the condition on `0x5991` and `0xe1f1` was not exercised from the menu.

11. **Recommended next actions.** T3b and T6 can import `work/scripts/pose.mjs` unchanged. `setPose('landscape' | 'exif6' | 'exif8')`, `deliver()`, then idle, then `read()`. Leave Orientation Linked's two rows checked when the caller needs indices 1, 3, and 5.

12. **Deviations from the brief.** The pose bytes are written only in the register-read hook. Four further emulator-only stand-ins are required because this emulator has no I2C bus and no data-ready pin: the pin-setup call is skipped, and bus open, write, and close return 0. They do not supply a pose. The native completion is called by the lab after that read, because the stock driver calls it from the unmodelled transfer path. No image was built. No storage choice. No hardware recommendation. Subject Detect was not enabled. NVRAM was off.
